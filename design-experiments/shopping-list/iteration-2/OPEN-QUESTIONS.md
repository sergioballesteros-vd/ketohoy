# Open questions

- Do shoppers identify a product faster in Flat or Zones? Compare identical lists with category headings as the only difference.
- Does `Necesitas` vs `Envase / Compra` make need and package clear within two seconds?
- For 39+ items, do category landmarks help more than a continuous stable order? Search remains out of scope.
- Does catalog price help the shopping decision, and is the detail disclaimer noticed?
- Do shoppers prefer bought rows to remain in place until reload, or want an explicit hide/clear affordance sooner?
- What should happen to long-session ordering after reload or when another tab mutates the list?
- Is the one-column desktop width comfortable without making the list feel sparse?
- Light background, global identity and physical one-handed use remain unvalidated.

The present checks are heuristic and automated, not a user study.
