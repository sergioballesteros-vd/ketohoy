# Price decision

- **Row:** secondary `Precio · 2,50 €` under the item metadata, only when the catalog product has a unit price.
- **Detail:** `Precio del producto en catálogo: 2,50 €. No es un total de compra.`
- **Total:** omitted. Package contents/count and missing prices make a sum easy to misread as the real basket cost.
- **Reason:** `Product.unitPrice` is the listed product's catalog unit price. It is not the recipe reference price and does not prove an actual checkout price or total. No approximate marker is used because no estimate contract exists.

Placement is technically grounded and visually secondary, but no user study establishes whether it helps or distracts: **NOT YET** as a reusable pattern.
