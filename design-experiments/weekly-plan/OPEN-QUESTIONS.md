# Weekly Plan — open questions

## Resolved for this experiment

- One-column order at desktop: yes; the sequence matters more than filling width.
- Images in plan rows: no; they do not help in the seeded current view and duplicate 28 times.
- Swap discoverability: actions disclosure with a visible “Acciones” accessible name; no swipe.
- Weekly purchase placement: quiet header action; no sticky bottom bar.
- Day navigation at 320 px: wrap 44 px targets into a 4+3 grid; all seven dates and Today are visible without horizontal overflow.
- Incomplete plan: show planned count and explicit empty meal slots, preserving the existing regeneration/preference recovery.
